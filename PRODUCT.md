# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static single-file HTML/CSS/JS (`index.html`), no frameworks, no tracking, system font stack. Hosted on GitHub Pages (`https://10elizabethbell.github.io/tmCleanOut/`). Set by Ellie's pitch-site pipeline, not asked per project.

## Users

- Homeowners in the Burlington County, NJ area who need a garage, basement or whole house emptied, a deep clean, or exterior work (power washing, deck staining, driveway sealing, painting). They find the link on Facebook, on a phone, inside Facebook's in-app browser. *(Inferred from the Facebook group the ad ran in.)*
- People responsible for vacant or foreclosed homes (realtors, property managers, families settling an estate) who need a cleanout, re-keying and winterizing. *(Inferred from "Clean out foreclosed homes" and "Re key/winterize homes".)*
- The owner, William Jones, is the first audience: the site is given to him free and must show his business, done well, on his phone.

## Product Purpose

The first web presence for T&M Clean Out LLC: one page that lists everything the business does, says who runs it, and gets a visitor to ask for a free estimate. Success: a visitor calls or texts William for an estimate.

## Positioning

One family-owned, fully insured crew that takes a property from full to empty to clean to painted and sealed, inside and out, including the jobs most crews skip (winterizing and re-keying vacant homes, boat bottoms, EP Henry paver sealing). Free estimates; discounts for military, first responders, teachers, seniors and new clients.

## Operating Context

- Contact today is by phone (two numbers) or email; free estimates. No booking system, no prices published.
- The owner posts sparse plain-text ads in local Facebook groups; "I don't post every job."
- Whether the numbers take texts is unknown.

- Service area: "Burlington County NJ area" inferred from the group; no towns stated.
- Hours, years in business, prices: unknown. Do not invent.
- Facebook business page "T&M clean out LLC handyman services" exists but its URL is unknown; the only public link is the group ad post.

## Product Principles

1. Everything he does, findable in seconds: the service list is the product.
2. A real person answers: name William, speak in his voice.
3. One way to reach him per screen; the estimate is free and the ask is small.
